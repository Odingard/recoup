4. FEES AND USAGE

4.1  Customer shall pay a base platform fee of Four Thousand Dollars ($4,000.00)
     per calendar month, invoiced in arrears, which entitles Customer to up to
     one million (1,000,000) API Calls per month.

4.2  API Calls in excess of the monthly entitlement set forth in 4.1 shall be
     billed at a rate of $0.0025 per additional API Call ("Overage"). Overage is
     calculated per calendar month and is not carried over or netted against
     months of lower usage.

4.3  The Term of this Agreement is three (3) years commencing on the Effective
     Date. No annual price adjustment applies during the initial Term.
