Recoup

Stop leaking revenue in your billing.

Recoup reads your contracts and your Stripe billing, then finds the money you should have collected — unenforced minimums, unbilled overages, expired discounts, and missed price escalators. Every figure is computed, never guessed.

Grounded findings

Each finding is tied to the exact clause it came from, with a confidence score. Low-confidence items are flagged for a human, not billed.

Read-only by design

We connect to your Stripe with a read-only key. Recoup never changes anything in your account.

Multi-tenant & secure

Firebase authentication and per-account isolation. Your data is never mixed with anyone else's.
