Privacy Policy

Recoup is a product of Odingard (Six Sense Enterprise Services) · Effective September 11, 2026

What we collect

  • Account information: your name and email from Google sign-in (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Business data you upload: contracts, invoice and usage exports, and corrections you make to extracted terms.
  • Connected billing data: if you connect Stripe, we read customers, invoices, invoice line items, subscriptions, and usage records with a read-only scope. We never write to your billing system.
  • Billing information for Recoup fees: your card is collected and stored by Stripe. We store only a payment-method reference, card brand, and last four digits.
  • Operational logs: request logs and an audit trail of actions taken in your account (approvals, recoveries, deletions).

How we use it

  • To extract contract terms, reconcile them against your billing data, and generate Findings, reports, and true-up documents for you.
  • To calculate and charge success fees as described in the Terms of Service.
  • To secure and operate the Service.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to train machine-learning models.

Processors we rely on

  • Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Firestore, Secret Manager, Vertex AI / Gemini) — hosting, storage, and contract-text extraction. Contract text is sent to Vertex AI for extraction under Google Cloud’s enterprise data terms, which exclude use for model training.
  • Firebase Authentication — sign-in.
  • Stripe — read-only connection to your billing data (if you connect it) and collection of Recoup fees.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account is active. You can permanently delete all account data — contracts, invoices, usage, findings, audit log, billing references, and any stored Stripe connection credential — at any time from the app (Settings → Delete account data). Deletion is immediate. Operational logs are retained for up to 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Billing credentials are stored in Google Secret Manager, isolated per account. Recoup’s own fee-collection key is separated from any key used to read your data. Access to production systems is restricted to Odingard personnel who need it to operate the Service.

Your rights

You can access, export (CSV/PDF from the app), correct, or delete your data at any time. For anything you cannot do in the app, email privacy@odingard.com. If you are in a jurisdiction with specific data-protection rights (for example the EU/UK or California), we will honor requests consistent with that law.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced by email or in the app.
