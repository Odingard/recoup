1. What the Service does

Recoup reads the contracts and billing/usage data you provide and identifies amounts that appear to be contractually owed to you but were not invoiced (“Findings”). Each Finding cites the contract clause it relies on, shows its calculation, and carries a confidence score. Items the Service cannot ground are placed in a Needs Review queue and are not reported as recoverable amounts.

Findings are analytical output, not legal, accounting, or tax advice. You are responsible for reviewing and approving each Finding before you act on it, and for deciding what to invoice your own customers.
