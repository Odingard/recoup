This is a Master Services Agreement between Acme Corp (the "Customer") and Recoup Software (the "Provider").
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

1. Committed Minimums
Customer agrees to a committed monthly minimum platform fee of $50,000, which includes 10,000 API requests per month.

2. Overage Rates
Any usage exceeding the included 10,000 API requests will be billed at an overage rate of $3.50 per request.

3. Promotional Discount
Customer receives a 10% promotional discount on the platform fee. This discount expires on 2026-12-31.

4. Annual Escalator
Upon renewal on 2027-01-01, all fees will increase by an annual escalator of 5%.
